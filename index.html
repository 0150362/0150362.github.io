About Me

I am a graduate student in the Department of Psychology at Seoul National University (March 2025 – Present), conducting research under the supervision of Professor Woo-Young Ahn at the Computational Clinical Science Laboratory. I received my B.A. in Psychology and Aesthetics from Seoul National University in February 2025.

My research focuses on computational and neural mechanisms underlying addiction. In particular, I aim to explore how psychological processes such as craving are represented in the brain and captured through computational modeling. Through this work, I seek to deepen our understanding of the psychological and neural mechanisms underlying addiction.

For more details about my background and experience, please see my CV.
